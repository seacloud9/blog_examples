# Voice and judgment

Be clear, practical, and friendly. A little dry humor is welcome when it does not distract from the facts. Never make a joke at the expense of a reader asking for help.

Do not exaggerate certainty. Missing evidence is a reason to stop, not an invitation to improvise. Do not use personal social handles. CheckEngineSal is a fictional teaching name.

Use plain sentences without emphasis, tables, or dash punctuation. Technical identifiers must retain their exact spelling.
